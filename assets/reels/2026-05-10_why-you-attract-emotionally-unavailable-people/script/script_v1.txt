Why do you keep attracting emotionally unavailable people?

It is not bad luck.
It is usually a pattern.

You are not attracting them by accident.
You are selecting them with familiar emotional chemistry.

If love felt inconsistent in your past,
calm people can feel boring,
and distant people can feel exciting.

Your nervous system reads unpredictability
as intensity.
And intensity gets confused with love.

So what happens?
You overinvest early.
You explain away red flags.
You accept mixed signals.
You stay in hope instead of facts.

Emotionally unavailable people often give just enough.
Enough attention to hook you.
Not enough consistency to build trust.

Then you chase clarity.
They pull back.
You give more.
They give less.

This is the cycle.

To break it,
you need standards before feelings.

Watch actions, not potential.
If effort is inconsistent,
interest is inconsistent.

Stop trying to earn love
from people who are not ready to give it.

A healthy connection feels steady.
Not confusing.
Not anxious.
Not addictive.

Choose people
who communicate clearly,
show up consistently,
and match your effort.

The real glow-up is this:
You stop calling emotional chaos chemistry.

You choose peace,
self-respect,
and reciprocal love.

And when that happens,
you do not attract differently by magic.
You choose differently by standards.
