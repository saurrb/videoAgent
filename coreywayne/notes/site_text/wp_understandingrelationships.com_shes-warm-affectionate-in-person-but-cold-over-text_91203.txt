Photo by iStock.com/LukaTDB
What it means and what you should do if your girl is warm and affectionate in person but cold over text.
In this video coaching newsletter I discuss an email from a guy who has been dating a woman who is sweet, affectionate and into him in person. However, over text she is cold and distant. She often takes a long time to reply to him. He’s gotten butt hurt over this and told her which is turning her off and makes her back away and make even less of an effort. He’s over pursuing and communicating that he doesn’t feel worthy of her and she is losing respect and attraction for him. I tell him what to do to turn things around. My comments are in
bold italics like this below
in the body of his email.
