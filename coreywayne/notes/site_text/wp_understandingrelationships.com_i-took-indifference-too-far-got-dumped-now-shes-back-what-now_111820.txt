How to balance indifference & pursuing to attract women back effortlessly.

Eventually she came back, but he wasn’t prepared & didn’t set a date. When she circled back he was ready, set a date and the Indoor Olympics happened. He’s unsure of how to balance pursuing and indifference going forward. My comments are in
