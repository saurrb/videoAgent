The Simple Cure For Women Who Jerk You Around
Apr 5, 2026 by
Coach Corey Wayne
Photo by iStock.com/Iqbal Nuril Anwar
A simple strategy to handle women who are inconsistent, flaky & jerk you around.
In this video coaching newsletter I discuss an email from a viewer who met a woman through his business that seemed interested. He made a date & then she canceled. Then she called a few days later and asked him out. The dates went well, but she blew him off once he got a little too over eager. Now he wonders what he can do to get her to be more consistent. My comments are in
bold italics
like this below in the body of his email.
Hi, I’m Coach Corey Wayne and this is my Video Coaching Newsletter. And the topic of today’s Members Only Newsletter is, “The Simple Cure For Women Who Jerk You Around”.
So this particular email is from a viewer who met his woman through his business. I guess he’s in the roofing business. She seemed interested, so he made a date with her and then she cancelled. And then a few days later, she reached out and invited him out to do something. So they went out. They had a good time, and then they made another date and she cancelled.
It seemed like he got a little overeager and she just kind of back and forth with this a little bit. So the girl’s displaying some flaky behavior, but she does seem to like him, so she might be a little bit of a screwball. But at the end of the day, it’s a good email to go through some of the strategies we talk about in
the book.
Because remember, dating is like tennis. You hit the ball over the net and you let them hit it back.
So what happens when a girl cancels at the last minute and doesn’t offer a reschedule, but then she calls you a few days later and asks you out? Now, the late, great Doc Love. His attitude was “one chance per girl per lifetime.” If she cancels a date with no offer or reschedule, you flush her number and you don’t ever go out with her again. I’m a little different. I like to give girls the benefit of the doubt.
And because at the end of the day, that’s what the viewers want. They’re like, “How do I get this girl?” Because she may be super hot and you may really like her and you’d like to at least hook up with her a few times, even if you don’t date her seriously and it doesn’t go anywhere. So you want to give yourself the best possible chance to do this.
Plus, you just want to get to the point where all of your interactions women are the same. You treat them all the same, and their behavior will determine whether or not they can stay in your life. Their compliance for treating you the way you want to be treated will determine whether they stay in your life or not.
Photo by iStock.com/BartekSzewczyk
Viewer Email:
Hey Coach,
So I’m near the end of a long divorce that has drained me the past few years. I recently have started getting back into dating and met a woman, she actually sold insurance to the roofing business I own, who has been divorced about 4 months.
So again, somebody right out of a breakup, right out of a divorce. You’ve got to understand, there’s going to be hot and cold. That’s normal behavior. That’s how I talk about in the book to warn you ahead of time. And, you know, so she’s going to be a little gun shy. We don’t know, he doesn’t admit or say what her marriage was like. Maybe it was a really bad maybe it was really traumatic. And maybe she’s still emotionally beat up over it.
So you have to assume she might come on and be really hot for you. And then all of a sudden cold the next week. And so this is the importance of understanding that dating is like tennis. You hit the ball over the net, you wait for her to hit it back. It’s very important as well that you let her come to you at her pace if you try to rush things, which he kind of did a little bit because he’s trying. He’s like, “Oh, I’m going out of town. I’d like to see you once more before I leave.”
I would never do that with a girl like this. It’s a bad way to go because she cancelled the date. When he did that, he tried to stuff more dates just because of his own time frame. Because he’s trying to make things happen a little quicker than he should. The idea as the book is set up, really, is to try to get you to go slightly slower than the women do.
When you go faster or try to go faster than the women are ready for, especially somebody that’s only been divorced for four months, you’re going to get blown off. You’re going to get rejected, you’re going to get canceled dates. So it’s important to understand how to operate when you encounter these situations.
Photo by iStock.com/momcilog
She gave me all the signs and kind of pursued me once she knew I was divorcing, and got a new job so I’m no longer her client. I reached out to her, asked her to dinner, originally she said yes, but then back tracked. I told her I understand and left it alone. A few days later she called asking me to a local event (Irish festival), and we went and had a great time. Ended up hanging out all day, lots of physical touch, and making out.
So in that case, if a woman asks you out or you ask her out, you make a date and then she cancels and you’re like, “Okay, no problem.” When or if she reaches out after that, she must bring up getting together. If she reaches out and is like, “Hey, how you doing?” “Oh, I’m great. How have you been?” You send no more than like three texts like that back and forth and you go, “Hey, it’s nice hearing from you. I’ve got to run, talk to you later.”
If she calls you, same thing. “Hey, it was nice hearing from you. I’ve got to run, talk to you later.” The only way you will ever bring up making a date or getting together is if she brings it up first, which she did in this case. So he did the right thing. He made a date.
Ended up hanging out all day, lots of physical touch, and making out.
So the girls clearly interested. But she’s going to be gun shy because she just got divorced.
I set up another date for dinner and she agreed, then the morning of she sent this long text about how she can’t do this, her feelings are too strong already, etc. I told her I understand.
That’s not a good sign. That’s a sign of a woman that’s fucked up.
Then a couple hours later she called me and said she still wants to go. We ended up having a great time, sushi, escape room, bar, and almost had sex in my truck before she said she wasn’t ready. I told her I understand and didn’t push the intimacy.
Photo by iStock.com/Dimensions
So you can see she’s bringing up getting together. She’s doing the right thing. After she backed out, you can tell she’s indecisive. You could say, “Well, it’s an attachment issue.” It’s just the girl’s a screwball. And so we don’t care about her attachment issues. We care about what creates and maintains attraction in a woman. So a woman, it’s a little more messed up like this one.
I mean, look how she behaves. That’s why there’s no need to lecture her. It’s just you respond based upon using the strategies that are in
3% Man
. Because even if she’s got attachment issues, like a Video Newsletter I did in the past week.
“How 3% Man Can Make Avoidant Women Purr Like Kittens”
.
I think it was something along those lines. So again, in this case, she cancelled. He’s not mad. He’s not butthurt. He’s like, “Hey, no problem. Hit me up if you change your mind.” And so she reached out and said, “Oh, I’d like to go.”
The next few days she was texting me very intimate things, very talkative, telling me how much she likes me, etc. but when I tried to set up a third date she hit me with the I’ll check my schedule. I told her I’d like to see her before I go on vacation next week, and she replied with “okay”. From there it went about 3 days where she didn’t interact at all, I sent a straight forward message asking her to dinner on Saturday and she again blew it off with no option of reschedule.
So technically what you did was you double texted. Remember, I say this a lot. You’ve got to give women the time and space to follow through on their plans and commitments, or to flake out and disappear forever. So if you knew you were leaving again, the book says you were going to initiate contact one date per week. But in this case, she was texting you. And so you say, “Hey, I’d like to see you before I go on vacation if you’re available.”
And if she says, “Okay, I’ll check my schedule.” I was like, “No problem, hit me up, let me know when you’re available.” And if she doesn’t reach back out to you, then you don’t reach out to her. You just let it be. And if we know she’s all over the ice like she has been, she told you she’s supposed to get back to you, so you give her the opportunity to follow through on that commitment or to flake out and disappear, potentially forever from your life.
Photo by iStock.com/momcilog
You don’t go, “Oh, I haven’t heard from her in three days. Let me call her again.” You’ve got to be congruent with your words. And that’s the one place where I’d say he slipped up here at the end, was not waiting for her to get back to him. Because that makes you look needy and impatient. And especially when you’re dealing with a girl with attachment issues or a girl that’s insecure or just out of a divorce. It’s not really her idea and you’re going to get rejected.
She again blew it off with no option of reschedule. I feel at this point I just go no contact and see if she reaches back out?
Yep. You shouldn’t have reached out at all. You should have let her get in touch or to never contact you ever again. But you know when you tell a woman like, because you’re thinking, “Oh, I want to get one more date and before I go, because I really want to move this thing forward. Maybe we’ll have sex before I go on vacation.” I’m sure that’s probably what he was thinking, “Because we were that close to my truck.” But you were a little impatient.
You over pursued a little bit. I know it’s not much, but that’s all it takes to get blown off once again. Especially with a girl that’s right out of a divorce. So at this point, you do nothing. If she reaches back out, say you go on vacation and you get back and she reaches out. “Hey, how you been?” Just say, “I’m doing great. I’d love to see you. What’s your schedule like?” And if she goes, “Oh, I’ll check my schedule and get back to you.”
I was like, “Awesome, let me know.” And then you never call or text her again. And the next time she reaches out, “Hey, what’d you figure out your schedule?” And she goes, “Oh, I don’t know.” I was just thinking about you. I want to see how you’re doing.” I was like, “Well, I’m doing great. Can’t really talk right now, but I definitely love to take you out when you’re available. Hit me up when you figure out your schedule.”
Photo by iStock.com/RealPeopleGroup
So you’ll ask two times when she reaches out and if both times she never brings up getting together. Then you stop asking. You don’t ask anymore until she brings up seeing you like she did for your first couple of dates. So that’s important. You were good for your first couple of dates, and they ended well because she did the right thing. She asked you out, and then the second one, she said she’d like to go anyways.
Maybe she apologized or not, but you can tell she’s kind of a scatterbrain. So you can’t pressure a girl like that at all, ever. Because you’ll get what you get. Which is she bounces and leaves you hanging. So you’ve got to go slower than she is. That’s the only real critique I had. Other than that, you handle yourself pretty well.
