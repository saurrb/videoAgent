The people who post the least
are often the hardest to fake.

Watch closely.

Some stop posting
because validation stopped working.
Likes feel loud.
Comments feel hollow.

Some stop posting
because silence protects depth.
Not every emotion
needs an audience.

Some stop posting
because identity stabilized.
They live life directly,
not through reactions.

So ask yourself:
Are you quiet from fear,
or quiet from clarity?

One silence shrinks you.
The other sets you free.
