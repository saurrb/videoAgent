Coach Corey Wayne

Some things to consider to determine what your girlfriend should pay if you live together.

They live in California where everything is expensive and he says it takes about $250,000 per year to live comfortably. She is old fashioned and thinks the man should pay for everything. He thinks it should be 50/50. She says she will break up with him if he doesn’t pay all the bills and asks my opinion. My comments are in
