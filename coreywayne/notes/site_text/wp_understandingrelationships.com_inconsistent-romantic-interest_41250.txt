my book

On the date she told him she thought he was not interested and that he only wanted friendship, because he was inconsistent and not looking for her everyday like other guys were who had romantic interest. He started reaching out more, and they have been dating and hooking up for a few weeks now, but he is unsure if he is contacting her too much and wants her attraction to grow and not wane.
