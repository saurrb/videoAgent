It is very easy to create an ideal image of someone in your mind and start believing that they are the perfect partner, even when their actions clearly show that the interest is not mutual. When emotions are involved, people often ignore the obvious signals and focus only on the small moments that give them hope.

The point about paying attention to actions instead of fantasies is very important. If someone truly wants to spend time with you, they usually make it clear and make the effort to meet. When that effort is missing, it is usually a sign that the feelings are not the same. Accepting that reality can be difficult, but it saves a lot of time and emotional energy.

It is also true that movies and stories sometimes give unrealistic expectations about relationships. In real life, healthy connections usually happen when both people show genuine interest. Moving on when the interest is not reciprocated is not failure, it is simply choosing to value your own time and emotional well being.
