Coach Corey Wayne
Photo by iStock.com/Antonio_Diaz
Some things to consider if you’re going to date long distance.
In this video coaching newsletter I discuss an email from a viewer who met a girl from another country 4 years ago and they hooked up, but he went back to his home country. He just got back from a recent trip to see her. She says she loves him, but was going to continue seeing other people while they were apart and then keeps asks him what are they? He asks my opinion on dating long distance and how to maintain the attraction. My comments are in
bold italics

And obviously goes to show, especially if you’re going to date somebody long distance, that’s like, you know, a plane ride away many hours.
