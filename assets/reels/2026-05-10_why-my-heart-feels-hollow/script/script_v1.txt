# Script v1 (2 min)

Why does your heart feel hollow?
Not because you are broken.
Because somewhere, you stopped choosing yourself.

You kept showing up for people
who stopped showing up for you.
You gave attention, time, energy,
and called it love,
while your own life got thinner.

A hollow heart is often a debt.
A debt created by self-abandonment.
Every ignored boundary.
Every red flag you excused.
Every time you said yes,
when your gut said no.

Then the breakup comes,
or the distance grows,
and suddenly you feel empty.
You are not empty.
You are uncentered.

Listen.
Pain is information.
It is not your identity.
It is telling you to rebuild.

Step one.
Stop chasing what is running away.
No essays.
No emotional bargaining.
No proving your worth.
Space is where truth appears.

Step two.
Reclaim structure.
Wake up earlier.
Train your body.
Clean your room.
Handle your money.
Finish your work.
Small promises kept daily
restore self-trust.

Step three.
Lead your mind.
If your thoughts spiral,
bring them back to facts,
action,
and mission.
A calm man is dangerous
because he cannot be manipulated by mood.

Step four.
Raise standards.
If someone disrespects your boundaries,
believe the pattern,
not the apology speech.
Love without respect is confusion,
not connection.

Step five.
Build a life so full of purpose
that loneliness no longer terrifies you.
When your direction is clear,
your emotions follow your lead.

And here is the shift:
The hollow feeling fades
when you stop asking,
"Who will fill me?"
and start asking,
"Who am I becoming?"

Become the man
who does not abandon himself
in the name of attachment.
Become consistent.
Become disciplined.
Become honest.

Then love becomes a bonus,
not a rescue mission.
And your heart stops feeling hollow,
because your life is finally aligned.
