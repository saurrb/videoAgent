Why you shouldn’t date and hookup with women who are in relationships with other men.
In this video coaching newsletter I discuss an email from a viewer who shares how he had a passionate one night stand with a woman who actually has a boyfriend. He is not thinking straight and seems to be suffering from captain save a hoe complex. He’s thinking she could be a friends with benefits and potentially a future girlfriend. She was already saying she wanted to have his babies during sex and he’s not seeing the red flags of her total lack of integrity. My comments are in
bold italics like this below
in the body of his email.
Why You Shouldn’t Mow Another Man’s Lawn To Steal His Girl
Hi, I’m Coach Corey Wayne and this is my video coaching newsletter. And the topic of today’s newsletter is going to be, Why You Shouldn’t Mow Another Man’s Lawn To Steal His Girl.
Well, in this particular email, this is a real gem. We’ve got a guy who has stepped up to the plate. He’s Mister White Knight. Mister Captain Save a Hoe. And so, apparently he had a one night stand with this woman who actually has a boyfriend. He’s obviously not really thinking straight and he’s thinking, “Hey, how do I make her mine? This is great!”
Because what do you see in the movies a lot? You see oftentimes a guy is with a girl and he’s really not that into her. And there’s this really beautiful, amazing girl. And she’s kind of with this guy that’s a douche. And the two of them meet, and he likes her. She kind of likes him. There’s chemistry there. And the whole movie is about; because neither one of them is happy in a relationship; how do you rip off the other dude’s chick? And then of course it happens. And then, they live happily ever after.
But in the real world, you know, this is how you get your ass beat. This is how bad things happen to you. I just saw, this was yesterday on Twitter. There was a guy who found out that his wife was cheating on him with somebody. And so, he went to wherever they were. And you can see this on video, it was on my it was on Twitter. It was it was shocking. And so, he shoots and kills her lover, and she’s screaming hysterically about it. And then he takes the gun and points it at his head and shoots and kills himself.
And so, she’s there with her lover and her husband dead. And it’s like, you know, that poor guy, the dude that did that, it’s like, that chick was not worth it. So, it’s like, that guy took his own life he took somebody else’s life. Instead of just saying, “Pfft. I’m going to replace your ass, I’ll find somebody better.” But instead, he had a scarcity mindset. He probably didn’t understand how attraction works.

And once that guy was dead and he realized, you know, he’s watching his wife just hysterical over her lover, and she really just didn’t give a damn about him. And then he kills himself. It’s like she doesn’t even care. And she goes back to crying and being upset over the guy that she was cheating on him with. It’s like it’s a horrible tragedy. I mean, that’s worst case.
And so, I’m telling you this because these things happen. You don’t want to be the dude trying to steal some other guy’s wife, because you don’t know what his mental state is. You don’t know what he’s thinking. It’s just not worth it. So, with that in mind, let’s go through his email.

What does that tell you? It’s the heat of the passionate moment. It’s like it was nice. It’s a nice thing. But don’t, don’t get drunk on that.

And that boyfriend would kick my ass if he found out.” I was like, “What was I thinking?” I was like, well, you probably shouldn’t drink too much and hang out with women who have loose morals and aren’t really loyal. Because you wake up the next day, and you’re like, “Damn, that was not my best moment. This is the type of thing you’re like, “Man, I just should not have drank so much the other night because I end up going home with this girl that’s got a boyfriend.” So how should you respond? I wouldn’t be responding at all. I’d be like, “Hey, you know, you have a boyfriend. You know, what we did was wrong. You know, I’m kind of mad at myself. You’re kind of irresistible. You took advantage of me when I was a little inebriated. It’s, I just don’t want to get involved with a girl that’s got a boyfriend.”
It’s like, “It shouldn’t have happened. It was wonderful, but it shouldn’t have happened, and I just. I can’t get involved with you.” That’s the right thing to do. That’s what a man of integrity is going to do. Even though you drank too much and end up sleeping with this other guy’s chick, it’s just not worth it. So, we have this. I’ve told this story many times over the years in other videos, but my younger brother, when he was in college, he was a valet guy, and one of his good friends was also a valet.
And there was this really cute hostess that worked at the restaurant that they were doing valet at. He had no idea. He was just over there thinking, hey, this girl likes me. She’s cute. Goes over there, and what happens? Kind of same thing like that happened on the Twitter video I was talking about earlier. Dude shows up, shoots to death, (my brother’s friend,) she’s freaking out. Again, it just happened recently that video was going viral on Twitter. And so, by the time you guys actually watch this video, it actually happened last week. It’s just horrible. It’s not worth it man. It’s like, when you have a book like this, find yourself a good woman who’s loyal and faithful.
