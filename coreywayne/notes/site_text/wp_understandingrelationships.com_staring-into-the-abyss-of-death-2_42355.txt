Staring Into The Abyss Of Death?
Dec 11, 2020 by
Coach Corey Wayne
Photo by iStock.com/Rawpixel
What you should do if you feel like you are staring into the abyss of death and life feels hopeless, so you can get through it and succeed like never before.
In this video coaching newsletter, I discuss an email success story from a guy who started following me about two years ago. He came home from a weekend trip to find out that his wife of eighteen years was leaving him and their two kids. He says he contemplated ending his life several times, because life seemed hopeless and he was in dire financial straits.
He shares how my work helped him to get through it and turn his life completely around. He is doing million-dollar real estate deals, started dating a new girl, his confidence is back and his kids are doing well. In these difficult times, it’s a great story to give you hope that no matter how bad things may seem right now, there is light at the end of the tunnel if you keep taking relentless, consistent action and moving towards what you really want in life. bold italics like this
below in the body of his email.

We’ve actually got an email success story here from a guy who had a really difficult time. He had some dark times in his his life, and he’s been following me for about the last two years. So, what happened was he came home from a trip and his wife of eighteen years was like, “See you later. I’m out of here.” She left him and his two kids.
Obviously, that was a pretty traumatic thing, but on top of that, he was in dire financial straits, and he admits that he contemplated ending it all numerous times. But at the end day, he had two young kids that he was responsible for and he’s like, “I can’t let my kids down.” And so, it’s a good success story of why you can’t ever quit, and you’ve got to put your head down and focus on the process of achieving your grandest goals and dreams.
And especially now, with everything that’s happened with all the lockdowns globally, because I’ve got clients all over the world. I mean, it’s just amazing what I just see in my own stats, whether it’s my YouTube channel or my website, just everything. You could see that human interactions globally ground to a halt. Now you’ve got different countries and different states locking down in the process.
I was walking down the street yesterday where I live, and there’s tons of retail space and restaurants. And the thing that’s shocking about the whole thing is there’s so much empty space now from businesses, whether it’s retail or restaurants, that just went out of business. And even when I go into the restaurants, because I’m in South Florida, this is the season when you get a lot of the snowbirds coming down here. Plus this time of the year, you get a lot of people come from all over the world to vacation here and stay here for the winter. And it’s like, I go in restaurants that I remember this time last year I was sitting in, and they were packed and they were on a wait. And now, they’re like ghost towns and it’s the middle of the season, which is crazy.
Photo by iStock.com/Gwengoat
As an American, it’s infuriating to see that, because these policies of the politicians have just basically put these people out of business, and they got a $1,200 check for their troubles. I mean, that’s completely unfair. I know millions and millions of people are hurting all around the world. And the people that seem to be wagging their fingers at everybody are people that are working from home, and they’ve still got their steady paycheck.
But everybody else, especially anybody in any kind of hospitality, restaurant or an entertainment type of industry, they’re out of work. Even the cruise ships, which I used to see all the time, coming in and out of the ports, because I could see them from my my building, it’s like, they’re not even there. They’re gone. And it’s like that.
That’s a lot of a lot of people. We’re talking billions and billions of dollars in cash flow, just like musical chairs, just stopped. And so, it’s easy to look around in society and go, hey, everything seems fine, but we don’t know the struggles that the people next to us, or maybe the person in the car next to us, or sitting in the table next to us, what they’re actually going through. It’s really difficult.
So, if you’re one of those people that has a good paying job and you’re working from home or whatever, you should definitely be counting your blessings, because a lot of people, millions and millions of people, have had their lives destroyed and their families destroyed. And when you get financial stress and you lose your business, it puts a lot of stress on the family.
One of the biggest reasons why people divorce and break up is due to financial stress. And so, this whole process, we have millions of families that are being destroyed. Millions of divorces are going to happen. Families are going to break up because of this, and people are going to be changed forever. A lot of people are going to give up on their goals and their dreams and never really stretch beyond their comfort zone, because of the traumatic experience that they’re going through right now.
