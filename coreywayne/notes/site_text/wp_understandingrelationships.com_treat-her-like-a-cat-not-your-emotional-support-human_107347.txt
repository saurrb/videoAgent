Treat Her Like A Cat. Not Your Emotional Support Human
Oct 21, 2025 by
Coach Corey Wayne
Photo by iStock.com/FG Trade Latin
Why you should treat your woman like a cat and not an emotional support human.
In this video coaching newsletter I discuss an email from a viewer who appears to be new to my work. He’s really struggling with his wife when she is aloof, distant and on her phone a lot. He gets anxious and worries when she is distant. He’s treating her like his emotional support human and mommy which turns her off.
I tell him what to do instead. My comments are in
bold italics
like this below in the body of his email.
This particular email is from a viewer who looks like he’s new to my work. He’s married and he’s really struggling with his wife because she gets aloof, distant, seems to be on her phone a lot at times. Other times she’s really she’s affectionate and the intimacy is great. So he’s kind of made it known that when he doesn’t hear from her or she’s busy doing other things, it really bothers him, which obviously is the wrong thing to do. He basically communicated he can’t handle it when he doesn’t get his atta-boys from mommy. You definitely should be reading
the book
, learning it and cleaning up your game. There’s a chapter in
my book
called Men Are Like Dogs. Women Are Like Cats. You have to understand that when you spend a lot of time with your woman, especially you guys live together, maybe you got kids, you should expect her to get a little bored and a little use to you if you spend a lot of time together.
So this is why it’s important to maintain your friendships, your hobbies, your interests and go to the gym, go visit your family, your mother, and have other things. So if she’s excited and warm towards you, spend time with her. If she’s a little busy doing other things, go hop in the car, go for a drive, go for a walk, or go for a run in the neighborhood. Go do something without her. Just say, “Hey babe, I’m going to go for a drive. I’m going to go for a run. I’m going to go have a beer with Bob,” whatever it happens to be. In other words, when she’s really sweet and affectionate, you’re going to spend time around her. When she’s a little cold and distant and doing other things, just kind of go do something else. You want her to be wondering, “What’s he doing? Where he’s at? When are you coming home, honey?” That’s what you want to see, but you can tell he’s looking to her for attention and validation all the time. He gets bothered and anxious when it seems like she doesn’t care as much.
You have to understand that’s just the way women are, especially around their period. They’re really hot for you. You spend a couple days together or a long weekend, and then it’s almost like Monday. She’s like, “Get the fuck out of here.” It’s like she’s less interested, and you can’t take it personally. That’s just the way they are. Their affection and their attention kind of ebbs and flows like the weather. You can’t take it personally.
Photo by iStock.com/Zinkevych
Viewer Email:
Hey Corey,
I’ve been following your work closely, and it’s helped me understand a lot about masculine energy and emotional self-control. I’m hoping for some perspective on my marriage.
My wife and I have been together for years, and we’ve had some ups and downs lately. She recently mentioned wanting a little more space and independence.
So that tells me you’re smothering her. That tells me you’re treating her more like your mommy, your therapist and your emotional support human, and you’re spending time around her and forcing yourself into her orbit, probably. Especially when she seems a little distant. You have to understand, as long as there’s no issues between you guys, you’re not butting heads on something or have something you need to talk about that, they’re just going to be that way. Under normal circumstances, when you’ve been together for a while and you understand this, you’re actually glad when she goes and hangs out with her girlfriends and does other things so you can do the same with yours. Where guys become a problem is when they notice that she’s distant, and then they seek reassurance from her that she still cares, which is very unattractive, very beta-like. So you got to knock that shit off. Seeking attention and validation is a feminine quality. It’s not a masculine one.
Women are attracted to confidence. Women like you more if they think that they are more into you than you are into them. So the fact that she’s asking for space means you’ve over-communicated your interest, and you’ve communicated that she has all the leverage, and you’re bothered by her absence or her lack of enthusiasm, and you can’t handle it. It shouldn’t bother you one way or another. You reward her good behavior and attention with more of your time and attention. If she treats you like a second class citizen or seems a little bored, go do things with other people. Let her wonder what you’re doing, where you’re at and who you’re with, because if she’s asking for space, that’s not a good thing. That means you’re driving her interest into the ground. So you gotta back off. You gotta let women come to you at their pace.
More time to herself or with her friends. At times, she’s affectionate, flirty, and loving, but then other times she pulls back, goes quiet, or seems more focused on her phone or work.
Well, that’s normal. That’s the way women are. Don’t take it personally. Their emotions are like the weather report, constantly changing. Especially for those of us who live in Florida, the running joke has always been, “Well, if you don’t like the weather in Florida, just wait 15 minutes because it will change.” Women really are like the same way, so don’t take it personal.
For example, we’ll have a great week with intimacy and connection, and then suddenly it feels like she’s distant again.
Probably because you become needy, you treat her like your mommy and you want her to give you an atta-boy, which is very unattractive. So when she does that, go do other things.
I’ve had anxiety about losing her or her pulling away, and I realize I’ve let that fear show through over-text or when I ask for reassurance.
Don’t ask for assurance, dude. Don’t ever fucking do that. A man does not need reassurance that his woman loves him.
I told her recently that I’ve been struggling with anxiety and needed her more emotionally…
Oh God, I can’t believe you said that! It’s horrible. Don’t fucking do that. You can say that to your guy friends or your therapist, but don’t dump that shit on your woman. It’s going to turn her off and dry her up. You’re just like, “Mommy. I need your attention. Mommy, do you love me?”
Photo by iStock.com/kieferpix
…But that seemed to make her pull back further.
Duhh, because it’s extremely unattractive. It’s not masculine at all. You’re acting like an insecure little girl when you do that. Women are most attracted to confidence. That’s their number one strength characteristic that they love in men that they’re most attracted to. When you basically tell her that you need assurance that she cares or likes you, it’s like, “Oh, I need you more emotionally,” don’t do that, dude. Your wife or your girlfriend is not there to be your emotional support human or to be your binky or your blankie. Don’t do that. Don’t ever fucking do that again.
Since then, I’ve been trying to center myself, give her space, and show strength through calm actions instead of words, like giving her flowers just because, being affectionate without expectation, and letting her unwind after work instead of chasing her for attention.
Well, I would be giving her flowers just because she’s being awesome to you. I wouldn’t be giving her flowers when she’s distant, because then that looks like a bribe for sex and attention, which is weak. You got to cut that shit out. A lot of guys do that. They get scared, or they have a problem or an issue with their girl and they’re like, “I’ll buy her flowers. I’ll buy her jewelry.” Don’t do that. Do that when things are really good, not when things are bad, because it looks like a bribe, which will dry her up.
The issue is that it’s hard to tell if I’m doing the right thing when she’s still distant for long stretches. For example, she might spend hours on her phone after work instead of engaging with me.
Well, that’s why you should go do something else if she’s like a little distant on her phone.
The other thing I gotta say is, if a woman is always on her phone, the phone’s always face down, or the phone rings and she goes to another room, usually those are the signs that she’s having conversations that she doesn’t want you to know about or hear about. So you should pay a little bit more attention to those things, but don’t freak out. Don’t say, “Are you talking to another man?” Or anything like that, because you don’t know what you don’t know yet. Scarcity creates value, and you’re too abundant in her life to the point where she’s taking you for granted. So when she’s like that, just go do something else without her. “Hey babe, I’m gonna go run some errands. Hey babe, I’m gonna go for a drive. Hey, I’m gonna go get the car washed. Hey, I’m gonna go hang out with Bob and have a few beers.” Just do other things
.
I’m trying not to take it personally or act needy, but it’s difficult when I feel the emotional gap.
Well, that’s part of being a man. You just gotta learn to be OK with it. You need enough time and repetition with what’s in
the book
to see her when she goes from being cold and distant, to in a few days, or maybe even a few hours, it turns around and then she comes back even more excited. It’s much better if she thinks that she’s way more into you than you are into her. The problem is, you’ve made it abundantly clear that you’re way, way, way more into her than she’s into you and you’re treating her like your mama and your therapist. That’s just going to make a woman just be like, “Oh, give me some fucking space, dude.” Don’t treat her like your mommy or your emotional support human, unless you want to drive her away.
What I’m struggling with most is how to stay strong, attractive, and emotionally centered when she’s inconsistent, affectionate one day and detached the next.
Well, that’s just the way women are, dude. You got to get used to it. Don’t take it personally. If you have daughters, they’re the same fucking way.
I want to lead the relationship, rebuild the connection, and be that calm, grounded presence she can relax into again.
Photo by iStock.com/laflor
Well, that’s why you’re going to have to relax. Go hang out with your guy friends. Go to the gym. Get caught up on your errands. Go do other things.
Any insight on how to navigate this balance between giving her space and still showing love would mean a lot.
Well again,
th
e
book
lays out when she’s ready to be touched, kissed, and seduced. If she starts bumping into you, touching you and playing with her hair, then you can slowly reciprocate affection, but wait until she gets to that place. If she seems a little distant, something’s on her mind, or she’s a little angry, just say, “Babe, you seem a little distant. You seem like you’re a little ticked off about something. Everything OK? Everything all right at work?” And get her to talk when you come home. “Hey babe, how was your day?” Get her to tell you. Open her up. When a woman feels heard and understood, the legs open. When she doesn’t, the legs close. She’s like, “I’ve done something. I need some space,” it’s like, “Hey, no problem.” Go do something and come back late.
I just want to get back to a place where the energy flows naturally again.
Thanks for all you do, Corey.
Bob
Well, you’re going to have to be patient, and you’re going to have to let her come to you. You got to stop treating her like your mommy, your therapist and emotional support human, because it’s extremely unattractive and it’s going to dry her up and it’s going to drive her away. I mean, you saw that when you told her you needed her more emotionally. Like, that’s repulsive. That is disgusting and repulsive to a woman. Don’t fucking do that ever again. It’s no bueno.
