# Hook
Love feels hard not because you're broken, but because you may be repeating a pattern that punishes your peace.

# Script v1 (Corey-only ~2 min)
If love keeps feeling heavy, confusing, and one-sided, this is your wake-up call.
Most people don't fail in love because they care too much.
They fail because they ignore reality too long.

You keep hoping potential will become consistency.
You keep over-giving, over-texting, over-explaining.
And every time you abandon your standards to keep someone, your self-respect drops.
That is when love starts feeling hard.

Attraction is not enough.
Chemistry is not commitment.
Words are not character.
A person shows who they are by patterns, not promises.

When someone is hot and cold, believe the pattern.
When someone avoids clarity, believe the pattern.
When someone only shows up on their terms, believe the pattern.

Love gets easier when your standards get stronger.
You stop chasing and start observing.
You stop forcing and start filtering.
You stop bargaining and start choosing.

Corey Wayne principle:
The strongest position is calm, centered, and willing to walk away.
Not angry.
Not dramatic.
Just clear.

If you're in a breakup phase, stop trying to negotiate attraction.
Give space.
Rebuild your body, mission, and mindset.
Get your emotional control back.
Your energy changes when your life is full again.

A confident man doesn't beg for consistency.
He requires it.
He doesn't fear losing people who cannot meet him.
He fears losing himself by tolerating less.

So if love feels hard right now, good.
Pain is feedback.
Raise your standards.
Protect your peace.
Master yourself first.
Then love becomes simpler, cleaner, and far more real.
