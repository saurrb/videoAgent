How to use playful banter to quickly get her number after matching online.
In this video coaching newsletter I discuss an email from a viewer who shares a short success story of how he used playful banter and stole one of my lines to playfully and quickly get a woman’s phone number after they matched on Facebook dating. I discuss the process to handle the phone call and set the first date after getting her number. My comments are in
bold italics
like this below in the body of his email.
Hi, I’m Coach Corey Wayne and this is my Video Coaching Newsletter. And the topic of today’s Newsletter is going to be, “How To Use Playful Banter To Get Her Number After Matching Online”.
Well, I’ve got a really short success story of a guy that got on Facebook dating and met this girl, bantered back and forth a little bit. As a matter of fact, he plagiarized, or I say he borrowed one of my lines that I use and actually had a pretty funny exchange with her, and she gave her number up pretty quickly. So it just shows how a little humor and a little playful banter can make it easy to quickly get a phone number.
Because with online dating, there’s a lot of time wasters, tire kickers and girls just looking and logging on for attention and validation because Chad Thunder Cock broke their heart. As soon as they get some of that, they’re off the app or they leave you hanging. And so you want to cut through the BS as quickly as possible in as few steps as possible.
Because once you get the phone number, you want to spend maybe ten, 15 minutes talking on the phone. And just so you can see how the conversation flows with her, take a sincere, authentic interest in the girl. Ask her about the types of things she loves to do and enjoy. Maybe you find out that you guys like doing the same thing, and that can help you plan what kind of a date you’re going to go on.
But the important thing is to find out. Is she fun to talk to? Do you like talking to or do you like to sound of her voice and vice versa? Because if your conversation is not flowing too good on the phone, it’s not going to typically flow any better in person.
So you’re trying to screen out the time wasters and the tire kickers from the girls that are sincere and obviously the girls that are the nuts and the lunatics that you should avoid at all costs. And if you can swing it, maybe even if the conversation goes really well, it’s like, “Hey, why don’t we switch to FaceTime?”
And then maybe you can switch to FaceTime. That way you can make sure that she looks as good on FaceTime video as she does in her pictures. Because again, when you go out on enough dates and enough online dates and you meet up with enough girls that don’t look like their picture, or you don’t really click with in person.
Photo by iStock.com/Rockaa
You want to have a good experience and you don’t want to waste your time, especially some of you guys that are on a budget. You don’t want to be wasting your time meeting girls for drinks or at a wine bar or whatever, and then you’re like, “”Shit, she doesn’t look like her pictures. Or I hate the sound of her voice. Or she’s just annoying and not fun to talk to.”
Because, you know, sometimes you can set a date where you don’t even talk to the person. And so the less you interact with them to pre-qualify them upfront, the more you’re just going to get a higher incidence of going out on dates with girls that don’t look like their pictures, you don’t like listening to them or talking to them.
So the more you can get done ahead of time, and any of you guys that are in sales that you deal with prospects yet, you know, you’ve got to go through a lot of leads in order to find a good one. And your time is limited. If you’ve only got 1 or 2 evenings available, you don’t want to be wasting those on girls that you could have weeded out over the phone or over FaceTime chat, because your time is valuable too.
