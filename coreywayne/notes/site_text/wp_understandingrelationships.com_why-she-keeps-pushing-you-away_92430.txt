Maybe he saw it, maybe he didn’t, but he went right back. The same exact behavior, a 50/50 pursuing. Again what happens? She backs off, gets flaky. “I’m overwhelmed.” It’s like, nothing changed. He didn’t hear from her for six weeks. She came back, and he’s exactly the same needy over-pursuing dude, bro. If you watch lots of videos, you hear me say you got to learn the manual here. This video is done with the intention for other people that are watching this who know
