Another Great 3% Man Success Story Of A Life & Relationship Turnaround
Feb 27, 2026 by
Coach Corey Wayne
Photo by iStock.com/milorad kravic
How 3% Man & Mastering Yourself can help you reach your full potential.
In this video coaching newsletter I discuss an email from a viewer & coaching client who shares how my work helped him to turnaround his personal and professional life since 2012. He shares how transformative my work has been across every area of his life that is important to him. It’s a 14 year history that demonstrates how anyone can transform their lives for the better. My comments are in
bold italics
like this below in the body of his email.
Hi, I’m Coach Corey Wayne and this is my Video Coaching Newsletter. And the topic of today’s Newsletter is going to be, “Another Great 3% Man Success Story Of A Life & Relationship Turnaround”.
So this email is from a viewer I’ve done phone sessions with, and he’s been following me for about 14 years. And so he really goes through how he’s basically taken
3% Man
and
Mastering Yourself
and completely transformed not only his personal life but his professional life. So some of you guys are going, “Who’s this shaved head dude on YouTube? What does he know? How can he actually help me?”
So here’s another great success story. And this guy’s been a student and an implementer of the things he’s learned from me. And he’s had great success. So hopefully it’ll inspire you or those you know who are struggling to read
the books
and master what’s in
Mastering Yourself
and so you can reach your full potential in every area of your life that’s important to you.
Because like I say all the time, even if you think I’m full of shit, this is why I give my books away for
free
so you can read them on my
Website
. You can try before you buy, and once you see these things work, then you can buy paperback and hardcover or
audiobooks
or book a
phone session
if you need to get my help one on one. Nobody else does that. Everybody else makes you pay for their books and their courses.
As a Coach, I’m all about self-reliance. I want you to get to the point where you don’t need me anymore. I’d say probably 95% of the phone sessions I do with people. It’s like one and done because they have a specific issue they need help with. That’s why I don’t sell coaching packages, and I’m not trying to do any of that bullshit. It’s just you’ve got a specific issue.
I’m happy to help you. I do have lots of clients that do come back over the years. Maybe once a year. They want to check in and just go through everything, which is fine. But the majority of people, they got a specific issue, they get help with it. And I like that, you know, I want them to not need me.
Photo by iStock.com/Prostock-Studio
I want them to learn this stuff so well, they can teach it to their own kids, their own family, their own friends, their own fathers, mothers, whatever it happens to be, because my goal is, I’m all about teaching self-reliance. And self-reliance is exactly that. Relying on yourself and not relying on me and getting big, giant, expensive coaching packages.
Viewer Email:
Dear Coach,
I wanted to reach out and send another heartfelt thank you for your work and the profound impact it has had on my life. I have been following your teachings since 2012, and the results across every area of my life have been truly transformative.
I was also the subject of two of your video newsletters,
“The Balanced, Centered & Successful Man”
and
“Success in Life & Relationships Is A Never Ending Process”
, and I’m incredibly grateful for the personalized guidance those provided, in addition to our
1-on-1 coaching
.
When I first found your work, I was in a very dark place. I had lost both of my parents and my daughter; my marriage was in jeopardy, I had no direction or purpose, and financially I was a wreck. Life seemed hopeless. Since then, everything has completely turned around.
Spirituality and Purpose. I have reconnected with my spiritual center and refined my vision and life purpose, which I now live from daily.
Marriage. Your work has been instrumental in our successful marriage and has helped me understand how to be the man my wife wants and needs me to be.
Health and Passion. My health and fitness are great; I’m back to riding and racing road bicycles and Indy cars.
Career and Wealth. My career is soaring as a leader in my field. I am continually courted by other firms for higher-paying roles.
Photo by iStock.com/Jacob Wackerhausen
This is what happens. You become a superstar. Headhunters hear about you and find out about you and they want to bring you to their clients.
Financially, we are debt-free, making a fantastic income, and have become multi-millionaires.
Well congratulations dude.
Legacy. My social life is booming, and I am teaching these success principles to my children, who are already applying them in their own lives.
Good fucking job dude.
I’ve learned that even with your tools, life can still get very tough.
It’s not all sunshine and roses. Life is a motherfucker. It will beat the fuck out of you. As soon as things seem like they’re going good, something else comes along. You know, one of the things that Andy Andrews said many years ago that I thought was so spot on. He says, basically, “The way life is, you’re either in a crisis, you’re headed for a crisis, or you’re coming out of a crisis.” That’s just basically how life is. And you just got to learn to deal with it. Because at the end of the day, you’re the one who assigns the meaning to the circumstances of your life. So make sure you choose meanings that are empowering instead of disempowering like most people do.
Making the conscious choice to adhere to these principles—and having the discipline to hold fast to them even when it seems like they aren’t working—is what truly makes the difference. I’ve found that your work isn’t just a list of things to do; it’s an identity and a lifestyle.
To ensure I stay on track, I developed a personal plan. I listen to a video every day. I downloaded for any open slots in my schedule, and I read from
How To Be A 3% Man
daily. I actually buy a new copy every year because I mark mine up so much they become barely legible by December.
Photo by iStock.com/Milan Markovic
This is a guy who is serious. He’s like the Tom Brady of life at this point because he’s constantly going back to the playbook, constantly rehearsing it, constantly trying to refine his approach and constantly trying to get better. He doesn’t act like a know it all. “Oh, I only read the book one time and I’m great.” He’s continually going through it because, again, we’re bombarded by propaganda in our society that basically corrals all of us into lives of quiet desperation and majoring in minor things.
I also continue to read
Mastering Yourself
daily. Your coaching was well worth the investment and had a huge impact.
Key principles that were game-changers for me:
Living from Mission and Purpose. This helped me transition into the leadership role I hold today.
Maintaining Your Center. Learning to never let external circumstances diminish my center has brought a level of stability I never had.
Abundance and Outcome Independence. These have been essential for my peace of mind.
Constant Self-Development. As you said in my video, it truly is a never-ending process.
Well as a man there’s always another level to evolve and grow to. When you get to the top of the mountain, you look in the distance and you realize, holy shit, there’s an even taller one to climb and conquer.
You and your work matter, and I can’t wait to see where you go from here—greater things are on the way. If I can ever be of help to you in anyway, don’t hesitate to reach out.
Thank you for your dedication to helping others reach their full potential. Keep killing it.
Best regards,
Bob
Photo by iStock.com/vladans
