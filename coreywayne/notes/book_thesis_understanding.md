# Book Thesis and Understanding

## How To Be A 3% Man (working understanding)
- Core thesis: attraction is primarily shaped by behavior, confidence, and emotional self-control rather than persuasion.
- Repeated principle: focus on purpose and mission first; relationships improve when your life direction is strong.
- Communication pattern emphasized: less over-texting, more direct dates, and decisive action.
- Attraction maintenance: avoid neediness, over-pursuit, and approval-seeking behavior.
- Frame control: boundaries, standards, and calm leadership are presented as key for long-term attraction.

## Mastering Yourself (working understanding)
- Core thesis: lasting relationship success follows internal alignment: values, discipline, and emotional mastery.
- Personal responsibility is central: your habits, standards, and routines determine outcomes.
- Identity-level change is prioritized over temporary tactics.
- Purpose-first model: health, finances, mission, and character are treated as foundational.
- Emotional regulation and delayed gratification are framed as force multipliers across life domains.

## Shared Themes Across Both
- Self-mastery before partner-management.
- Mission and masculinity as central organizing ideas.
- Directness, confidence, and consistency.
- Boundaries and respect as prerequisites for healthy dynamics.
