If She Likes You, She’ll Make It Easy To See Her
Nov 17, 2025 by
Coach Corey Wayne
Photo by iStock.com/id-work
Extending invitations to women who are out of town or passing through so they chase you.
In this video coaching newsletter I discuss an email from a viewer who got interviewed about his work from a gorgeous woman while he was traveling. She approached him and later hit him up on social media after the event. She will be in his town in a few weeks and he extended an invitation to get together when she is. He asks me to critique his approach. My comments are in
bold italics
like this below in the body of his email.
Hi, I’m Coach Corey Wayne, and this is my Video Coaching Newsletter. And the topic of today’s Members Only Newsletter is, “If She Likes You, She’ll Make It Easy To See Her”.
So this is an interesting situation here. This particular guy, he got interviewed by a really gorgeous woman about his work while he, I guess he was traveling. He was at a trade show or something like that, or some kind of event. And so she approached him, wanted to interview him, and she seemed to really be into his work, and he seemed like there was a little chemistry there. And obviously he thought she was pretty. And then she reached out to him on social media.
And so they started chatting back and forth. And so I guess apparently she’s going to be in his town in a few weeks. And so he extended an invitation to get together when she’s there. And so he wanted me to critique his approach. And so the reason why I chose this email is
the book
the way it’s laid out is it’s designed to go to create the conditions where you’re going slightly slower than the woman is. And when you do that, then it causes her to have to work to get your attention and interest. And the reality is, women like you more if they think that they’re more into you than you are into them.
And so typically, the goal under normal circumstances is that it’s true that the man’s got to start the courtship. You get her number, you set a date, and then usually by a second or third date, second or third week since you guys met. Most women are going to sleep with a guy by the second or third date in the West. And when that happens, typically she starts reaching out after that. And then when she does that, then you just use that as an opportunity to set the next date. And as the weeks go by, she calls and texts more.
And then by week seven, week eight, she’s pretty much at your house just about every night, or vice versa. You’re at her place every night. It just kind of naturally comes together like that. So the guy starts out making one date per week, and then the woman, when her interest goes up, starts calling and texting and FaceTiming you, two, three times a week. And when that’s happening, there’s no reason it’s actually counterproductive to continue reaching out, because this way she stays away as long as she feels she wants to. And then when she starts to miss you, she wants to bond and connect, she’ll text you, she’ll reach out.
Photo by iStock.com/bmanzurova
And that’s just women are natural pursuers. They do this naturally and innately. If you get a group of women together and ask them, they’ll go, oh no, I want the man to pursue me. Men are supposed to be the pursuers. It is true in the beginning. But what happens is when the woman’s interest goes up and her respect goes up, and she wants to bond and connect and be in a relationship, she’s just going to be trying to get his attention. More women want your attention all the fucking time when they’re in love with you, to the point where it gets pretty annoying.
But that’s the way they are. And if you treat them properly, they’ll treat you properly. And it makes it really easy as a man, when the woman is doing most of the pursuing and wanting to connect. Because again, women are designed to get our attention. So in this particular situation here, this guy meets a woman. He does some kind of work to where he’s like the authority. So in other words, he’s the desirable guy that all the women want. So I think he does something he says in the creative industry. So I don’t know if he’s an entertainer. He’s an artist, a sculptor, whatever it happens to be.
So people are looking up to him. And this woman obviously is really into his work. And so therefore, even though he hasn’t been on a date or anything, she’s already pursuing him. She’s already chasing him. And if you’re applying what’s in
the book
enough, and you’re developing your social life enough and you’re doing great in your mission, your purpose, women are going to hear about you and they’re going to seek you out and they’re going to come and hunt you down, which is kind of like what’s going on here. So even though they haven’t been on a date or anything yet, she’s already pursuing and reaching out to him.
So what normally happens in most cases, 2 or 3 weeks after you meet, she’s already doing it. She’s already doing pretty much all the reaching out first. And so in that case, you don’t have to do anything. You just let her reach out and then you make dates. So it’s a good email from that perspective. It’s fun when these things happen. And it’s also just shows the woman’s got a high level of interest in this guy based upon her actions. And so there’s really no reason for him to even pursue. If you meet a woman and she’s already pursuing you, it’s counterproductive to start pursuing her. Because you’ll notice if you start doing that, then as the weeks go by, she’ll start pursuing you less and then you’ll find yourself doing most, if not all, the pursuing.
Photo by iStock.com/m-gucci
A lot of guys that have been in this situation with a girl that’s doing all the reaching out at first, they’ll reach out to the girl and things will be hot and heavy for about 2 or 3 weeks, maybe four weeks, and then what happens is she starts backing off. And as she backs off, he pursues more to the point where she starts going, “oh, I’m confused. I’m not sure I feel overwhelmed.” You’ll get stuff like that. And so this is like, if you guys ever played T-ball as a kid, it’s like, the ball’s right there and all you got to do is swing and knock that thing right off the tee, which is pretty cool. So how do you handle that situation?
So that’s why I wanted to go through this as a Members Only post, because it’s one of those little subtle nuances of how to apply what’s in
the book.
Because women are natural pursuers, and most of the time the guy’s going to have to start the pursuit in the beginning. And then at week 2 or 3, the woman kind of picks the ball up and really runs with it. Whereas here they’ve met, she’s reaching out to him, and so she’s already pursuing him. So there’s really no reason for him to even have to pursue her. He can wait to hear from her and make dates.
Makes it really nice, really easy. And once you understand that, you won’t fuck it up, basically. And I think everybody watching this is probably one of those experiences where they met a girl who was really into them at first, like this, doing all the pursuing, and then 3 or 4 weeks down the road, she’s not doing any pursuing, and then you’re doing all of it. And then shortly after that she dips, and then you’re going, what the hell?
