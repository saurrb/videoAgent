Things to consider if you just broke up with a gaslighting cheating woman.

He’s clearly new to my work and got completely bamboozled by her. It’s a good email to learn from that demonstrates why people don’t change. Once a cheater always a cheater. My comments are in

They may become a better version of who they are, but they typically don’t change. Once a cheater, always a cheater. And what did Maya Angelou say? “When somebody shows you or tells you who they are, believe them the first time.” So character is destiny.
Somebody that’s going to look you in the eyes and lie to your face about what they’re doing and then later confess it. If you take them back, then you just validate that it’s okay to live that way. It’s okay for them to be that way. The only thing that could potentially cause them to realize that it’s not the best way to be is if every time they cheat or screw around, or incorporate some kind of fuckery in how they’re treating the guy they’re dating.
They get dumped permanently and they get no second chance. But there’s always another beta male sucker that’s willing to step up to the plate and put up with this, thinking he’s going to change a girl because he saw too many Disney movies. It just doesn’t fucking work. This chick was broken. She’s 33 years old, broken by her family, and it’s not your job to fix her or to save her. But you got to see reality as it is. Because, again, you give a woman like this a second chance, she’ll just screw you over again, which clearly happens here. And now he’s licking his wounds.
But I’m really glad he sent this in, because there’ll be other guys that are in this position, or they’re thinking about giving a woman like this another chance. And when they see an email like this, they’ll be like, “Holy shit, I think I’m gonna pass on the second trip through crazy town. Thank you very much.” Let somebody else have that fun.
