How to casually pickup & date a woman so it leads to a relationship.
In this video coaching newsletter I discuss an email from a viewer who’s been out on several “dates” with a woman with too much butler and man servant behavior. He hasn’t escalated anything physically. She even refers to him as a friend. He doesn’t know what to do to escalate physically so I explain how to effortlessly transition to physical intimacy and the signs he needs to look for to know she’s ready.

3% Man

As a woman’s interest goes up, she’s going to call you more, she’s going to text you more, she’s going to FaceTime you more, she’s going to send you more memes and pictures and want to be in contact with you. The more she likes you and the more she’s reaching out, the more you’re making dates. The more you’re making dates, the more you’re hanging out, the more you’re having fun, the more you’re hooking up.
If you’re following what’s in
the book

Then you can have those nice discussions because she brought it up. Your job as a man is to create the opportunity for sex to happen. To hang out, have fun, and hook up. When she’s feeling comfortable, she’s going to be the one to bring up bonding and connecting or being in a relationship.
So with that in mind, this particular guy, he’s been on several dates with a woman, but it just looks like he’s been a little too much of a butler and a man servant. He hasn’t really escalated anything physically. When a guy does this, eventually she figures out he’s got no game, doesn’t know what he’s doing, and then she sticks him in the Harry Honda category or the friend-zone category, and it gets really hard to get out of once she’s put you there. the book
