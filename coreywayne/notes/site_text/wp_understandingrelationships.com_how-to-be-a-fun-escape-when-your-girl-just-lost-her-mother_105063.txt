How To Be A Fun Escape When Your Girl Just Lost Her Mother
Apr 8, 2025 by
Coach Corey Wayne
Photo by iStock.com/Hispanolistic
How to be a fun escape from immense pain after your girl loses her mother.
In this video coaching newsletter I discuss an email from a coaching client whose wife just lost her mother. She’s taking meds to cope and has started smoking again. She is distraught and having a hard time dealing with the loss. Sex and intimacy have dried up.
He wants to know what he can do to help her heal and have fun together after being a caregiver 24/7 for the past six months. My comments are in
bold italics
like this below in the body of his email.
Well, at some point in everybody’s life, especially when you’re in a relationship, the person you love is going to lose somebody that’s really close to them.
This particular email is from a coaching client of mine whose wife just lost her mother. For the last six months, she was her caretaker. She’s basically spent 24/7 being her nurse for her end-of-life care, basically until she passed away. So now his wife has been like three days taking meds to cope, she’s taken Xanax, I think he said. She started smoking again, which he doesn’t like. Also, sex and intimacy is kind of like she’s probably not that interested in that. He’s like, “Hey, you got some videos that I can watch that would really help me help her, help my wife?
It’s a good topic because this is going to happen to everybody, because this is life, man. Everyone you love, everything you build, eventually it’s going to turn to dust. If you’re going to be in a relationship with somebody else, eventually you’re going to watch them lose people that they love and that they’re close to, and it sucks, but that’s life, man. It’s all fucking tragedy because it’s like we’re all on the Titanic. We’re like on the stern of the Titanic. Some people are a little further down, a little closer to the waterline, so they’re going to go underwater and die first before us and we kind of watch all that. At the end of the day, we’re still in the stern of the ship, and eventually the stern of the ship is going to go under.
So what can you do before the Titanic completely sinks? How much fun can you have? How much love can you have? How much love can you share? What can you build? What can you do? What can you experience? How many laughs can you have? How much love can you share? That’s what you need to focus on because it’s a tragedy. It’s everything’s going to turn to dust, so you might as well find a way to enjoy your life.
Photo by iStock.com/LightFieldStudios
Viewer Email:
Hi Corey,
We have spoken privately before.
My wife’s mother just died three days ago and she was very close with her. Basically her mum had cancer and my wife was by her bedside until her death day and night for the last six months. She was the closest to her than the other two sisters.
Well, one thing I have to say about that, having watched this happen in my own family and people really close to me and other people in my family be a nurse to their significant other, the reality is, most of the time the other family members don’t really want to be involved. The fact that your wife was basically her mother’s caretaker for the last six months and spent 24/7 night and day taking care of her? She’s an angel. The fact that her other two sisters, maybe they’re too busy or whatever, but that’s usually how it works. There’s usually one person that does everything for the mother, the father or whoever. Maybe it’s a sister, maybe it’s a family member, maybe it’s a child in some cases.
I can’t remember the video I did several years ago. It was probably at least 10 years ago at this point, but a guy was one of my followers and I think his son was a follower also if I’m not mistaken, and his son died suddenly. Was a car accident or something like that, and his son was like only in his 20s. I think he was he was a young guy, but there were like hundreds and hundreds of people that came to his funeral. I mean, he sent a detailed email and it was pretty amazing for his son to be that young and so many people that loved him showed up to see him off. It’s like, as a dad, how could he not feel proud of that? It’s like man, he raised a son that so many people loved and missed. They showed up. So that’s something to be proud of because he was really struggling with it. Losing a child like that, it’s awful.
So these things are going to happen. That’s just life. It’s just a matter of time. So in this case, the fact that she’s willing to take care of her, again I’ve watched other family members of mine take care of their parents. One of them in particular, took care of her mother for last three or four years of her life, did everything for her, washed her, bathed her, wiped her butt, cleaned her up, cut her hair, made sure she was fed. She had dementia and she just kept fading away. It was just interesting, she was really old too, to watch her kind of regress to like a child, basically where she’s wearing diapers and stuff like that. She had many siblings and none of them really wanted anything to do to help, but you know, as soon as mom died, they were all wanting to know when they were going to get their money for their inheritance, which is sad, but that’s usually kind of how it works in most families. It’s like the people that love and care for the person the most are the ones that are there. The rest of them, they love them, but they just don’t want to be involved. They don’t have the bandwidth or the time, but the fact that your wife did this? She’s an angel. You need to let her know.
The fact that she did this for her mother, took care of her mother, did everything for her in the same way the mother did for her when she was a little baby and an infant and raised her like, that’s really sweet. You need to let your wife know how grateful you are that you’re married to somebody that is willing to do that when the rest of the people in the family couldn’t be bothered. Let’s be honest, they couldn’t be bothered, and it’s sad, but you know, that’s kind of what happens. You get to the end of your life, you realize who really cares and who doesn’t.
My wife has lost it and is on all different meds to cope. Saying to me, “I’m cleaning so mum likes it when she comes home.”
In other words, pretending like she’s there. I would assume in spirit she’s probably still there, lingering. She’s at peace now. When my own mother died, she came to my brother in a dream, I think it was about a month after it had happened. She’s like, “I’m all better now,” and it’s like, “Oh by the way, you got a sister you need to take care of,” which turned out to be her female dog, a little puppy she had in the house.
Plus, now she started smoking again, but also told me she needs pills to cope with the loss. Xanax. Also, I don’t think she wants any intimacy, but it’s early days.
What videos of yours do you recommend for me right now?
Thanking you in advance!
Bob
Photo by iStock.com/elenaleonova
Well, what is the basic formula? Your job in the courtship is what? To create an opportunity for sex to happen. To hang out, to have fun, and when signs are there that she’s ready to be touched, ready to be kissed, ready to be seduced, to hook up. That’s what you need to do.
So I would approach this and just say, “Honey, I’m really sorry that we lost your mom. She was an angel, and you’re a fucking angel for taking care of her and doing all the things that you did for all that time, and the fact that nobody else in your family was willing to do that. Nobody else was really willing to come to her aid. You know what? We need to celebrate your mom’s life. Celebrate all the great things that she did and she left behind. Obviously, you’re here, and every parent, what parents care most about is that their kids grow up to be happy and healthy and enjoy their lives. We can’t do anything for your mother now. I mean, you did all this work for the last six months, and now it’s time for us to have some fun, to have a little vacation away from this so we can go and celebrate her life. Yes, we could be sad that she’s gone, but let’s go celebrate her, because more than anything, I’m sure she would want to know that you’re happy, that you’re having fun and that you’re loving your life. That would make your mom most proud.”
“So even though this is a tragic time and this is sad and we’re all bummed about losing her, the best thing we can do to honor her and her memory is to enjoy life. So let’s go away for the weekend. Let’s go do something fun. Let’s go to bed and breakfast. Let’s do a stay-cation in the city.” Maybe you can’t get away or whatever. Do a stay-cation for a couple of days. “Let’s go to the beach, just hang out, listen to the sound of the ocean and the birds and just relax. Let’s have a few cocktails. Let’s go have a good time.” I know you don’t like the fact that she smoked. It’s like, “We’ll even pick up a carton of cigarettes on the way to where we’re going.” Obviously, smoking is not healthy, but she’s coping.
What you need to be is her man, her escape. You need to be the guy to snap her out of it, because it’s like when you’re on a date, if you dwell on negative things or all the guys that broke her heart or didn’t treat her well, you’re eliciting negative feelings in her. Whatever you make a woman feel when she’s with you is what she’s going to associate with being with you. In this case, just going and doing something fun together, hanging out, having fun, and when the signs are there, to hook up, and she will love you and she will appreciate you for the fact that you did this. You just made things happen. You make a trip happen. You make a weekend getaway happen. You sneak off to the beach or the lake, or maybe the ski slopes. Maybe you guys are into skiing or maybe just going for a hike in nature. Pray, meditate, hang out. Just enjoy hanging out together. Don’t dwell on it. Just keep her laughing and having a good time and take her mind off of things because she spent the last six months basically watching her mother deteriorate and slip away, and she was powerless to do anything other than make her mother feel comfortable and take care of her.
Just tell her, “You deserve to have a good time. So let’s go have fun this weekend. Let’s celebrate. Let’s make this weekend getaway or week long getaway,” whatever you can do and you can afford. “This weekend, our mission is to celebrate your mother and her life and to show her, because she’s watching down on us from heaven, to show her that we’re going to celebrate her life and we’re going to be grateful for all the wonderful things that she’s done because she left behind a wonderful family, obviously a great daughter like you, because you took care of her. The last thing she wants to do is see you laying around bumming and feeling like shit, popping pills and smoking cigarettes. Maybe we’ll have a few cocktails, smoke a few cigarettes or whatever, lay on the beach and let’s just go enjoy ourselves, relax and find a way to have a good time to honor your mother. That’d be the best way to celebrate her, to have a celebration that goes on for the whole weekend.”
Go hang out, have fun and hook up. Keep your wife talking. Focus on positive things. Just like on a date. All the basics from
the book
apply in this situation. Hang on, have fun, hook up. It’s pretty simple. Be her escape. Obviously if she wants to talk about her mother or cry, hold her, comfort her and then go do something fun together because you got to feel it to heal it. That will be helpful to her. So when she needs to cry or get upset, hug her, tell her how much you love her, how proud you are of her and what a great daughter she is, what an amazing daughter, how she was the only one in her whole family who was willing to take care of her mother. She deserves a break. She deserves to have a good time over the next several days as a job well done to help her mother throughout her last days, to be a good daughter. How proud can you imagine that her mother would be to think, “Man, my daughter took care of me, wipe my butt, cleaned up everything, bathed me,” did everything that was necessary at the end of her life. What a sweet thing. What a great mom her mother was and what a great daughter your wife is. Focus on those things.
Photo by iStock.com/Drazen Zigic
So your wife deserves to have a good time this week and just focus on that. Focus on doing fun things and interesting things. Yeah, she’ll probably break down and get upset, but if you keep her focused on having fun, hanging out, exploring and do fun things together. She’ll love you for that and tell her how grateful you are to have her as your wife and how grateful you are to know that she’s loving and nurturing enough that she would do that for her mother, because quite frankly, a lot of people just send grandma and grandpa to the retirement home, and they let people that don’t give a shit about them take care of them, and nobody’s there. Nobody comes to visit. It’s like you took care of her and that’s really sweet. Most people would not do that. Your wife deserves to have a good time and you’re going to show her that good time.
So make it happen. Escape for the weekend. Do a bed and breakfast. Go to the beach. Go to the lake. Go to the mountains. Hike. Whatever. Spend time in nature. Go do fun things. Go do something you guys have always wanted to do for the weekend and just make like a little vacation. The whole vacation, the point of it is to celebrate her mother’s life and also to celebrate your wife, what a great wife she is, what a great daughter she was to her mother and how proud you are of the fact that she did all of that all those months when pretty much nobody else didn’t do jack shit for her, because that’s usually how it works in most families. You know, she could have just dumped her in the hospice care, left her at a retirement home, gone to visit a couple of times, but no, she took care of her mom and did everything for her, just like her mother did when she was a little kid, an infant. Did everything for her. So that’s pretty awesome! Congratulations to you for marrying such a good woman that would do something like that, because that’s hard. It’s not easy. It’s not fun. It’s not something you want to do, but you do it because it’s your duty.
Again, you made a great choice, dude. So congratulations on your great choice! Congratulations to your wife for being a great daughter to her mother! You guys have nothing to celebrate. I mean, what a job well done. That’s what you got to look at. This was a job well done, and the only person in the world that could pull this off and to do it was your wife. So that’s a reflection upon your wife, her character and your character for selecting her and marrying her. So go have a fucking blast this weekend, celebrate her mother’s life and have a good time!
